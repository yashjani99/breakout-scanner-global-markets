## Common Mistakes & How to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Trading ALL Signals
```
Dad sees 15 signals on Monday.
He trades all 15.
Result: Some poor quality signals drag down performance.

✅ SOLUTION:
Only trade Quality > 75 (maximum 3-5 per week)
Better 3 good trades than 15 mediocre trades.
```

### ❌ Mistake 2: Ignoring Stop Loss
```
Dad enters RELIANCE at 2800.
Stop Loss is at 2720 (Risk ₹80).
Price drops to 2725.
Dad thinks: "I'll hold, it will recover"
Price hits 2600.
Dad loses ₹200 instead of ₹80 (2.5× more)

✅ SOLUTION:
SET STOP LOSS IMMEDIATELY after buying.
Don't negotiate with losses. Exit if SL is hit.
Period.
```

### ❌ Mistake 3: Moving Stop Loss to Loss
```
Dad enters at 2800, SL at 2720.
Price drops to 2740.
Dad moves SL to 2750 (worse).
Price then hits 2740, but SL already at 2750.
He loses ₹50 extra due to "adjusting" SL.

✅ SOLUTION:
Set SL before buying.
Only MOVE it higher (to lock profits).
Never move it lower (that's called "hope trading").
```

### ❌ Mistake 4: Not Taking Profits
```
Dad buys INFY at 1900, T2 at 1980.
Price reaches 1980.
Dad thinks: "Might go higher!"
Price drops back to 1920.
Dad exits at 1920 (didn't take profit).

✅ SOLUTION:
Take profits at T1 or T2 (that's the PLAN).
Don't get greedy. Consistent profits > home runs.
```

### ❌ Mistake 5: Changing the Rules Mid-Trade
```
Entry rule: Quality > 75
Dad sees Quality 62 signal.
He thinks: "This looks good anyway"
He trades it.
It loses.

✅ SOLUTION:
WRITE DOWN the rules.
Follow them exactly.
No exceptions, no "but this one looks different."
Rules exist for a reason.
```

## Reading the GUI Table

### Column Guide

When you see results in the app:

| Column | What to Look For |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Stock** | Ticker symbol |
| **Quality** | 75+ = Trade, <75 = Skip |
| **Trend** | Strong Uptrend = Good |
| **SL** | Set stop loss here (non-negotiable) |
| **T1, T2** | Take profits here (follow the plan) |
| **RSI** | 50-70 = Good (not overbought >80) |
| **ADX** | 20+ = Strong trend |
